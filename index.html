The Ruleset

What is a Nuzlocke?

A self-imposed Pokémon challenge designed to make every decision count.

01

First encounter only

On each new route or area, you may catch only the first Pokémon you encounter.

02

Fainted means retired

If a Pokémon faints, it is permanently boxed or released and cannot be used again.

03

Hardcore adds limits

Hardcore runs commonly ban items in battle, level caps, and over-leveling before major fights.

Hall of Fame

Pokémon Run & Bun

Currently considered to be at the pinnacle of RomHack difficulty alongside Platinum Kaizo. It is extremely tedious to nuzlocke, with every single enemy team incorporating some sort of competitive strategy. In mid to late game, planning just one fight can easily take an hour or more, requiring the player to do many calculations to know which Pokemon to bring.

Hall of Fame

Emerald Kaizo

A Generation 3 nuzlocke that used to be infamously known as the hardest RomHack to Nuzlocke before eventually being dethroned by Run and Bun. There were many balance changes applied to various Pokemon (some revamped entirely), contributing to its steep learning curve.

Hall of Fame

Radical Red

A difficulty RomHack based on Pokemon Red, it featured a revamped enemy AI, making it much smarter and as a result harder to plan fights. It embedded Pokemon and mechanics all the way up to Generation 8, making nuzlocking it overwhelming and challenging. Furthermore, enemy teams were all competitively viable and had good held items. With the E4 and Champion having multiple legendaries at their disposal, Radical Red truly put itself up there as one of the most difficult RomHacks in history.

Trainer Profile

About pChal

Jan Krüger (b. 1995) streams and uploads as pChal on Twitch and YouTube, where his channel is built around Pokémon Nuzlocke challenges — a fan-made ruleset that permanently retires any Pokémon that faints and limits the player to the first encounter in each area. He is candid on-camera about the grind behind his runs, which is part of what has built a loyal following around his content.

His three most talked-about clears are Pokémon Run & Bun, Emerald Kaizo, and Radical Red — ROM hacks built specifically to punish Nuzlocke players with fully reworked, over-leveled trainer teams from the first gym onward.
