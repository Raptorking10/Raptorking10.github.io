About pChal

Jan Krüger (b. 1995) streams and uploads as pChal on Twitch and YouTube, where his channel is built around Pokémon Nuzlocke challenges — a fan-made ruleset that permanently retires any Pokémon that faints and limits the player to the first encounter in each area. He is candid on-camera about the grind behind his runs, which is part of what has built a loyal following around his content.

His three most talked-about clears are Pokémon Run & Bun, Emerald Kaizo, and Radical Red — ROM hacks built specifically to punish Nuzlocke players with fully reworked, over-leveled trainer teams from the first gym onward.
